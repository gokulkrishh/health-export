Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Health Export lets you export your Apple Health data to a file on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app reads, what it keeps, and what it never does.

Summary

Your health data stays on your device. Health Export has no servers, no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It never sends your health data anywhere. A file only leaves the app when you share it yourself.

Health Data Access

Health Export reads Apple Health data through HealthKit. Access is read-only: the app never writes to or changes your Health data.

When you first use the app, it asks once for read access to every type it can export (about 190). These cover activity, body measurements, cycle tracking, hearing, heart (including ECG summaries), mental wellbeing (State of Mind), mobility, nutrition, respiratory, sleep, symptoms, vitals, workouts, other data, and characteristics such as date of birth, biological sex, blood type, skin type and wheelchair use.

You decide what to allow in the iOS permission sheet. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Health Export.

How Your Data Is Used

All processing happens on your device. Health data is read only to build the export you ask for. You choose the data types, the date range, an optional source filter (only data from the apps or devices you pick), the detail level (every sample or a daily summary) and the format (JSON or CSV). Each export includes the name of the app or device that recorded each sample.

Exported Files

Each export is written to the app's temporary folder and protected with iOS Data Protection (complete protection), so it can't be read while your iPhone is locked. The file is shared only through the iOS share sheet, and only when you choose to. It is deleted as soon as the share sheet closes, and any leftover files are deleted the next time the app launches.

Once you share a file, where it goes (Files, AirDrop, another app or anywhere else) is outside Health Export's control. That destination's own privacy practices apply, so share health data only with people and services you trust.

Data Stored on Your Device

Health Export stores a few settings in the app's local storage (UserDefaults). None of it is health data:

  • Whether you've completed onboarding
  • Which data types you've selected
  • How many exports have succeeded, and the app version when a rating prompt was last shown. These are used only to decide when to show Apple's standard rating prompt.

This information never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Ratings

Health Export may ask you to rate the app using Apple's standard StoreKit review request. Apple controls whether the prompt appears and handles any rating you give.

Purchases

The Unlock Full History purchase is made and verified through Apple's StoreKit. Apple handles payment; Health Export only learns whether the unlock is active and never receives your payment details or Apple Account information.

Network Activity

Health Export has no servers and sends no data anywhere. The only network activity is Apple's own App Store service, used by StoreKit to show the price and handle the unlock purchase. The app also leaves when you tap a link to the App Store (such as the rating page) or open the Health app or Settings.

Children

Health Export is not directed at children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age where you live.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
