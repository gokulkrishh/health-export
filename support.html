Support

Need help with Health Export? Most questions are answered below.

An export says “No Data Found”

iOS doesn't tell apps when Health access is turned off; they simply get no data. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Health Export and turn on the data types you want, then export again.

Some older data is missing

When you allowed access, iOS may have offered to share only recent data. In the same Settings screen, choose to share all recorded data.

Where does my file go?

Each export is created on your iPhone and opened in the share sheet. Save it to Files, AirDrop it or open it in another app. Health Export deletes its own copy when you close the share sheet.

Contact

Still stuck? Open a support request and include your iPhone model and iOS version. Please don't include any health data.
